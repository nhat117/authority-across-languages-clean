# LLM usage statement

OpenAI GPT-6.1 Sol through OpenCode assisted with the experimental code,
multilingual prompt variants, analysis checks, and drafting/editing the report.
The reported GPT-5 experiments used `gpt-5-2025-08-07` through Braintrust to
produce worker and monitor replies. These replies are experimental data, not
human participant responses.

Numerical findings were calculated from saved outputs using evaluation scripts.
Original replies and invalid cases remain preserved. Failed-request records and
the complete historical audit trail remain in the linked original archive.
Unsuccessful results were not replaced to improve the findings.

The separate sampled judge workflow saved 288 replies for 96 randomly selected
groups. Of these, 286 passed the rubric and exact-evidence checks; two remain
instrument failures. There are 94 provisional PASS groups and two PENDING
groups. This is sampled LLM-based assessment, not full-corpus or native-speaker
validation. The worker and judge share the GPT-5 model family and may share
errors or preferences.

The final authors must check and approve the text, references, analysis and
conclusions before submission. AI assistance does not replace author approval
or independent human review. This clean repository only performs offline
reanalysis; it makes no new LLM calls.
